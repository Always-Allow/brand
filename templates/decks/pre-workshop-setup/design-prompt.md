# Claude Design build prompt: Before We Start

Paste this into Claude Design along with `outline.md` and the Always Allow brand assets from
`brand/`.

---

Build a one-page setup guide from the attached copy. Use the copy verbatim. Do not rewrite it, do
not add sections, do not pad it with encouragement.

**Format**

- One page, portrait, sized for both screen reading and printing.
- It gets read on a phone as often as a laptop, so nothing should need zooming.

**Brand**

- Coral `#D85A30` for accents and the numbered step markers.
- Dark `#1a1a1a` for body text.
- Beige `#f4f3ee` background.
- Always Allow lockup at the top. `AlwaysAllow.com` in the footer.

**Layout**

- Title and the one-line intro at the top, given real space. That intro is what makes people
  actually do the steps, so it should not read as fine print.
- Five numbered steps down the page. Each step is a short heading and one or two lines. The
  number is the visual anchor, large and coral.
- The Windows note (step 4) gets a light callout treatment so Windows users find it while
  scanning. Do not make it alarming, just findable.
- "The night before" checklist sits at the bottom as real checkboxes with enough room to tick by
  hand on a printed copy.

**Tone rules**

- No em dashes anywhere. Commas, periods or parentheses instead.
- No exclamation marks.
- Plain English. The audience is smart and non-technical, and several of them are nervous about
  this. Calm and matter-of-fact beats enthusiastic.

**Do not include**

- Screenshots. They go stale every release and this page has to survive a year.
- A "what you'll learn" section. This page has one job.
