# Before We Start: Setup

One page, sent to attendees (or the client contact) a few days before any workshop. Generic on
purpose so it works for a public session or a private client engagement. Swap the bracketed bits.

---

## PAGE 1

### Title
**Before We Start**
*Five minutes of setup so day one is all building*

### Intro line
You do not need to prepare anything or know anything going in. You do need Claude installed and
signed in, because we start building in the first twenty minutes and there is no time to fix
logins in the room.

---

### 1. Install the desktop app

Claude runs in a browser, but the desktop app is what we use. Download it at **claude.ai/download**
and install it like any other app.

- **Mac:** open the file and drag Claude to Applications.
- **Windows:** run the installer and accept the defaults.

### 2. Sign in and confirm you are on the right account

Open the app and sign in. If your organization has a team or enterprise workspace, sign in with
your **work email**, not a personal one. A personal account will not see shared projects or
anything your team has set up.

**How to check:** click your name in the bottom left. If you see your company name, you are on the
team workspace. If you only see your own name, tell [CONTACT] before the session.

### 3. Confirm the features are turned on

Open the app and look for these. If any are missing, it is an admin setting, not something you
can fix yourself, so flag it early.

- **Cowork** (works with your files and tools)
- **Design** (builds slides and documents)
- **Connectors** or **Settings, Connectors**

### 4. Windows users only

Cowork needs one system setting enabled on Windows. If you do not see Cowork in the app, send a
note to your IT contact before the session rather than waiting until the day.

### 5. Bring a charged laptop

Not a tablet. There will be power in the room, but start full.

- [ ] Cowork and Design both visible
- [ ] Laptop charged
