The inventory prompt

Run it in a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT, outside a project, so it can use your memory and your past chats. It comes back with the things you already do over and over.

Look at what you know about me: your memory of me, my past chats if you can look at them, and any skills you can see that I have. Just reply here in the chat. Don't search the web and don't create a file.

  1. List things I do over and over, numbered, one line each. No more than five. Each one is a single piece of work, like "turn meeting notes into a follow-up email", not a whole area like "marketing". Include things I still do by hand, not only things I have already built something for.
  2. End each line with one of these in brackets: (already built), (partly built), (not built), (not sure).
  3. If you know very little about me, don't guess. Ask me these three questions in one message, then make the list from my answers: What did you do this week that you also did last week? What do you copy and paste between apps? What do you keep putting off because it's tedious?

Keep the list under 100 words. Then stop, and don't ask me anything else.
