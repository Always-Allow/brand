## 1. The provenance rule

Every color, font, treatment, and rule here traces to something Sarah chose, measured, or
approved, and the source is named. No one, Claude included, may introduce a new color, font,
texture, mascot, illustration style, or visual metaphor that is not in this document or approved
by Sarah after it. Numbers are measured, never asserted: any contrast ratio, size, or alignment
claim gets checked in a browser or tool before it is written down.

`[2026-09-02]` This rule is also the precedence rule: no document earlier than this guide is a
source for anything visual, whatever it says, other than the sources this guide names in its
opening paragraph and anything Sarah approves after it; and this guide outranks FACTS Part D on
visual matters as recorded on 2026-08-27.

## 2. Color

One light theme. No dark treatments anywhere, on any surface.

**One carve-out, her ruling 2026-08-31.** Dark source files may be kept in `brand/` so a dark cut
exists if it is ever needed. They are held, not used: nothing dark is placed on a page, a slide,
a deck or any other surface without her saying so first. `2026.08.27_SymbolBone.svg` is the file
this covers. Keeping it is not permission to reach for it.

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| canvas | `#FEFDFB` | The floor of every page and slide. Near-white, not pure white |
| bone | `#F0ECE6` | Cards, bands, chips. A surface color, never the floor |
| bone-deep | `#E7E0D4` | Deeper bone accents |
| hairline | `#E3DDD2` | Borders and rules |
| ink | `#344650` | Text. The visibly slate blue, never darker |
| muted | `#427294` | Secondary text, on canvas only |
| accent (Petrol) | `#1D5372` | The one accent. Never as text: fill behind bone type, or a marker under ink type |
| chip-gold | `#F3E5C2` | The warning/scheduled chip |

Measured contrast: ink on canvas 9.66:1, muted on canvas 5.08:1, canvas on Petrol 8.16:1, all
pass. Muted on bone measures 4.39:1 and FAILS, so muted text never sits on bone.

Color encodes the kind of thing, not the individual item; four unrelated pastels on four cards is
the rejected pattern. White is not a second brand color (exception: printed interiors on white
stock). Objects standing in for other products carry that product's real colors; this palette
governs Always Allow surfaces only. See section 6, which is where that rule does most of its work.

## 3. Depth

Three shadow steps, slate-tinted: rest, raised, float. The rule that gives them meaning: vendor
objects float on a shadow; Always Allow's own objects do not, they sit on a hairline. That
difference carries information, so shadows are never decoration. Occlusion beats fading:
something disappearing behind something else reads as depth, a fade reads as a slideshow.

## 4. Type

- Inter is the working face: body, UI, labels, navigation. Identity comes from heavy weights and
  tight letter-spacing.
- DM Serif Display on display headlines only (her ruling 2026-08-27, chosen from a live
  side-by-side; this amends the earlier all-Inter ruling 30). Big headlines and the brand name in
  hero positions get the serif; everything else stays Inter.
- Three heading levels, no fourth.
- No monospace anywhere, with one exception she asked for by name: a skill rendered as a slash
  command, mono on a pale Petrol chip, because there it should look like the tool. Never widened
  to filenames, labels, or body copy.
- The closing note on `/friendlies` is a one-page exception approved by Sarah on 2026-09-13 after
  a readability revision: its message uses DM Serif Display and its signature uses Caveat.

### Site layout contract, added 2026-09-13

The site kit is `site/assets/system.css`. Its layout variables and shared classes are the built
source of truth, not starting suggestions to be restated inside a page.

- The page shell is 1160px with 48px total viewport gutter.
- Artifact stages are 1120px and sit on the same center line as the page shell.
- The navigation is 92px at rest, with 26px of vertical padding.
- The recurring vertical rhythm is 12px for copy, 24px for controls, 48px for rows, 64px for
  openings, and 104px between major sections.
- There are three content heading roles. Display is DM Serif Display at 54px to 62px. Section is
  Inter at 26px to 34px. Item is Inter at 27px, with 20px available for supporting labels.
- Body copy uses a 62ch measure unless the content itself requires something narrower.
- Workshop photo strips use four 4:3 frames at desktop with a 16px gap, two frames at tablet, and
  one frame on a phone. The shared default preserves the full image instead of cropping it.

Pages own section order, copy, artifact arrangement, and interaction. They do not own alternate
shell widths, navigation dimensions, type scales, or a new spacing scale. If a page genuinely
needs another layout role, add and document that role in the kit first, then consume it from the
page.

### The grammar

One shared standard, opted into per workflow. Three steps, there is no fourth:

1. Load `workflow-standard.css` and `workflow-standard.js`.
2. Put `wf-std` on the stage with a name: `<div class="stage shape-yours wf-std"
   data-workflow="Lead Routing">`.
3. Delete the page's own boundary script; the shared one replaces it and two will fight.

**The skeleton is free, the visual language is fixed.** Each workflow arranges its own boxes,
because a funnel, a fan-in and a fork are different jobs and should not look alike. Everything
else is shared, and changing it for one workflow changes it for all of them. The four-column
template that made three different jobs look identical is dead.

The stage is a fixed 1120x500 canvas scaled by `--stage-scale`.

### The color contract for cards

Every card sets its vendor color inline as `--evidence-rgb: R,G,B`. Everything else derives:

    --edge: color-mix(in srgb, rgb(var(--evidence-rgb)) 76%, #2b2018);

Color rides the EDGE (a 4px offset slab), never the fill. Faces are white, the ground is tinted,
never the reverse. No glass and no backdrop-filter on cards; inner strips use opaque fills. Raw
brand colors are hotter than this palette, which is why they are mixed 76% toward the warm dark
before use. A mark on a filled chrome sits on a solid white tile, because these logos are drawn
for white; the exception is a logo that is already an opaque tile (HubSpot), where the bar
matches the asset instead.

**Vendor colors here are correct by rule, not a palette leak.** Her standing ruling is that
objects standing in for other products carry that product's real colors. The parts that are hers, and must match her tokens, are
the ground and the card face.

### Standing rules for any artifact

- Everything inside a card arrives at once. Nothing staggers in on its own animation.
- A stage caption finishes typing before its card lands, by 0.28 to 0.33 seconds, measured on the
  running page, never calculated.
- The boundary is one rounded path drawn by its own dashoffset, not four bars, and its label comes
  from `data-workflow`, never hardcoded. The travelling glow is retired.
- Hover lifts a card and grows its slab; a card becomes hoverable when the connector leaving it
  starts to draw, so cards go live in the order the work runs.
- No isometric tilt. These are nodes in a flat flow and tilting breaks every connector arrowhead.
- Numbers inside a drawn card ("24 unread", "38 mentions") are illustration, not business claims,
  and are exempt from the no-hardcoded-numbers rule.
- Where no vendor mark exists, draw it (the PDF slip is drawn because radar-assets has no PDF
  mark). Never substitute a lookalike.

## 7. Motion

Approved vocabulary: stage beats arrive as one (caption, arrow and card together); card groups
change over by sharpen-in (blur to sharp, staggered); objects occlude rather than fade. The
hand-tuned timing lives in `homepage.css` and the workflow standard, and those are the reference
implementations. Teaser copy is three or four words.

The homepage band rotates seven source-and-result pairs, each with its own app tiles, arriving in
four rotating compositions with fresh per-card offsets and tilts so it never reads as a loop.
OpenAI appears deliberately in named places (the drafting skill row, one band pair, the fork
scene's two marks) so the site is not anchored entirely in Claude; the morning brief stays Claude.
