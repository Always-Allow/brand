# Always Allow Asset Manifest

Snapshot updated 2026.09.03. The repository is the source; the design-kit ZIP is the portable download.

## Brand system

- Approved visual and voice guides.
- Portable design guide plus CSS and JSON tokens.
- Inter Regular Latin and DM Serif Display Regular Latin, both WOFF2.
- Locked logo system: master, lockup, wordmark, avatar, symbol, and small/tiny cuts in SVG and PNG.
- Canonical site system stylesheet.

## Platform graphics

- Etsy: 500×500 opaque shop logo and 1600×400 shop banner.
- Mercury: 1024×1024 JPEG logo.
- LinkedIn: company logo, company cover, and personal banner.
- Slack: workspace icon and sidebar theme values.
- Luma: calendar icon, cover, and social image.
- Notion: seven light 2400×460 covers and seven 512×512 page icons, with SVG sources.

Etsy and Mercury are regenerated and checked by
`brand/Platforms/2026.09.02_PlatformLogoBuild.mjs`. Notion is regenerated and checked by
`brand/Platforms/Notion/2026.09.02_NotionAssetsBuild.mjs`.

## Templates

- 14-page slide deck template: `workshops/templates/always-allow-deck.dc.html`.
- 17-slide editable PowerPoint deck kit, including approved keycap backgrounds and
  replaceable tech-stack rails: `workshops/templates/always-allow-deck-kit.pptx`.
- Two-page US Letter workshop one-pager: `always-allow-one-pager.dc.html`.
- Six-page copyable visual-elements library: `always-allow-visual-elements.dc.html`.
- Seven approved workshop-cover compositions with HTML sources and PNG previews.
- PDF, template, and preview builders under `workshops/templates/build/`.

## Image and icon libraries

- 157 current tool marks under `site/assets/tools/`, copied from the public radar-assets master.
- Current photography library and its manifest under `site/photos/`.
- Current share card, favicon, cursor cuts, and site image exports from `site/assets/`.
- Current canonical artifact-library HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and tool index from `site/artifacts/`.
  Retired artifact pages are deliberately excluded from the portable kit.
