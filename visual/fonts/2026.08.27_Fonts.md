## Why they are here rather than loaded from the web

`2026.08.27_BannerBuild.mjs` in the parent folder embeds a typeface into the banner it renders, so
that the banner cannot come out differently on a different machine. Without the file in the
repository that script depends on something outside it, and nobody else can reproduce the banner.
The same applies to any print or offline work.

`[2026-09-27]` The obsolete folder-history paragraphs were removed whole. The current font and builder locations are given at the top of this document; git history preserves the earlier wording.

## What these files cover

The Latin subset only, which is what the site and every current asset use. For a wider character
set, download the full families from Google Fonts:
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Serif+Display and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

## The wordmark is DM Serif Display

Measured 2026-08-27, not assumed. Every letter width in `2026.08.27_Wordmark.svg`, taken as a
ratio to its own cap height, matches live DM Serif Display within half a percent, which is the
rounding noise of the measurement: A 0.9509 against 0.9508, l 0.3623 against 0.3626, w 1.1472
against 1.1469, a 0.7358 against 0.7370, y 0.7774 against 0.7797, s 0.5774 against 0.5784, o
0.7472 against 0.7508.

That means the wordmark CAN be re-set from live type if it ever needs different spacing, tracking
or extra characters. It is committed as outlines anyway, which is normal for a wordmark: outlines
render identically everywhere and do not depend on a font being present.
