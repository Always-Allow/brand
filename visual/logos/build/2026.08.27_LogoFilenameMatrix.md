## Usage rules

- The two-glyph keycap never ships below 48px. Under 48px the small mark replaces it, always.
- At 16px the small mark is replaced in turn by `2026.08.27_LogoMarkTiny.svg`. The small mark is
  correct at 32 and 48 and fails at 16: measured on the retired `LogoMarkSmall-16.png`, only 2
  pixels in the whole image were true bone against 98 at 32px, and the centre counter closed to a
  2px blend at 2.10:1 where 32 and 48 both render it as clean Petrol at 7.05:1. Scaling is not the
  remedy and was tested: the mark's proportions are fractional at this size whatever the scale, so
  every wall and counter straddles a pixel boundary. The tiny cut redraws the same symbol on the
  pixel grid, one pixel per wall and two per counter, and its builder renders it and fails unless
  all five counters come back as flat Petrol. It is a 16px cut and nothing else: measured, it
  gives 56 flat bone pixels with 4 blended at 16px, against 28 flat with 116 blended at 20px, so
  do not reach for it at the sizes in between.
- The master's viewBox was extended on 2026-08-27 from 393.8 to 413.8 units so it stops cutting
  off its own ground shadow, which was leaving a hard horizontal edge across the bottom on every
  ground including white. Nothing about the construction moved: the whole diff to the master is
  that one number and every path is byte identical. The bound is the shadow filter's own region,
  so rendering at 413.8, 420 and 460 units all put the last drawn row in the same place.
- The lockup's box is the artwork, with no built-in margin. It used to be a 510x90 canvas that
  left the drawing filling 53.5% of the width and 41.7% of the height, so sizing the file by its
  height gave a logo at 42% of the size asked for, 6px off centre. Clear space is applied at
  placement, per the guide's 8%-of-the-short-edge rule, rather than baked into the file.
- The dark master is source only. It ships nowhere and nothing derives from it, because the guide
  bans dark treatments on any surface. The plan's direction is that dark variants retire, and
  Sarah's 5b.2 confirmation decides only whether the retired files are deleted or kept as history.
  `[2026-09-02]` Answered: Sarah confirmed on 2026-09-02 that the dark master should be deleted, and
  `2026.08.27_LogoMasterDark.svg` is gone. The bullet above is left as written on 2026-08-27.
- Glyph placement inside the masters is part of the locked construction and is accepted by that
  lock, not by a centring measurement: the face is a perspective quad whose bounding-box centre is
  not its visual centre. Recorded in the plan's 5b.6 addendum.
- The wordmark sets the company name as two words, per ruling 5. Sarah chose the wide space on
  2026-08-27 from four rendered options. The face is DM Serif Display, measured rather than
  assumed: every letter width as a ratio to cap height matches the live font within half a
  percent. The font is in `brand/fonts/`, so the type can be re-set if it ever needs different
  tracking or extra characters.
- The lockup preserves three measured relationships from the lockup it replaces: key height
  29.746094, gap 18.055983, and the key centred on the wordmark's full ink extent. The key is
  placed by its keycap body rather than by its viewBox, because the viewBox carries shadow padding
  and the visible key fills only 91.45% of its width and 84.34% of its height.
- Colour provenance, per the guide's rule that every colour names its source. The masters carry
  `#14384D`, `#1D5372`, `#344650`, `#C2CBD1`, `#F0ECE6`, `#FFFFFF` and five rgba values; the dark
  master swaps `#1D5372` and `#14384D` for `#3C7FA2` and `#2A5E7C`. `[2026-09-02]` One master
  now: the dark one is deleted, and the generator's dark switch, which made that swap, is unused.
  `#1D5372`, `#344650` and
  `#F0ECE6` are guide tokens. Every other value is a constant of the build() generator, covered by
  the 2026-08-27 construction lock. The lock's scope is the six readout numbers above and every
  constant inside build(). A separate record of that scope sat in the planning folder and went with
  the 2026-09-01 planning clear; the generator itself is tracked beside this file as

## Optimisation rule

None, deliberately. Generated files are committed exactly as their builder emits them, byte for
byte: no minifier, no optimiser pass, no hand edits. The small mark is committed unmodified and
pinned by hash. Committing is not shipping; what ships is governed by the usage rules above. Any
commit touching these files must first pass every builder's own check:

    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_LogoExport.mjs --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_WordmarkBuild.mjs brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_WordmarkSource.svg brand/logos/2026.08.27_Wordmark.svg --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_LockupBuild.mjs brand/logos/2026.08.27_LogoMaster.svg brand/logos/2026.08.27_Wordmark.svg brand/logos/2026.08.27_Lockup.svg --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_AvatarBuild.mjs brand/logos/2026.08.27_LogoMarkSmall.svg 500 brand/logos/2026.08.27_Avatar.svg --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_SymbolBuild.mjs brand/logos/2026.08.27_LogoMarkSmall.svg ink brand/logos/2026.08.27_SymbolInk.svg --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_SymbolBuild.mjs brand/logos/2026.08.27_LogoMarkSmall.svg bone brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_SymbolBone.svg --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_MarkTinyBuild.mjs brand/logos/2026.08.27_LogoMarkTiny.svg --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_BannerBuild.mjs brand/logos/2026.08.27_Lockup.svg brand/fonts/2026.08.27_Inter_Regular_Latin.woff2 brand/Platforms/Etsy/2026.09.02_EtsyBanner_1600x400.png --check
    node brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_RasterExport.mjs --manifest brand/logos/build/2026.08.27_RasterManifest.json --check
    node brand/Platforms/2026.09.02_PlatformLogoBuild.mjs --check
    python3 brand/Platforms/Slack/2026.09.25_PipelineAgentAvatarBuild.py --check

`2026.08.27_WordmarkBuild.mjs` reads `2026.08.27_WordmarkSource.svg`, the outlines the wordmark
was cut from, kept in this folder so the builder can run. It is a source file, not a deliverable,
and nothing ships it. It still carries the retired ink colour `#2D2926`, which is correct: it is
the file as it was, and the builder is what re-inks it. Do not "fix" it.

**What the checks do and do not prove.** Each `--check` compares a committed file against what its
builder produces now, which proves the two agree. That alone would not catch a builder that was
wrong from the start, so each builder also asserts properties of its own output: the exporters
check references resolve, no live text, no embedded raster, no empty glyph wrapper and no retired
colour; the wordmark builder checks every glyph origin and that the root has no intrinsic size; the
lockup builder reads its own emitted markup back and re-derives the scale, the gap, the centring
and the box from the attributes it actually wrote, because a verifier that checks the numbers it
has just computed proves only that the arithmetic agrees with itself; the banner builder renders,
measures where the content landed, corrects it, renders again, and refuses to write at all unless
the result is centred on both axes to within a pixel; the tiny cut's builder fails unless every
one of its five counters renders as flat Petrol; the avatar and symbol
builders re-extract the outline from both files independently rather than trusting one shared
match; the raster exporters read the PNG back and fail on a blank render. The banner's typeface is
pinned by hash, because a font that silently falls back cannot be detected from the pixels: this
tagline measures 305px wide with the intended font and 295px with the fallback.

`[2026-09-02]` Those two widths were measured on the 1200x300 banner. The 1600x400 banner's tagline
measures 364px and its builder pins that, with a bound of 13.

## Raster export

Use `2026.08.27_RasterExport.mjs`, which renders through a browser at exactly the pixel size asked
for and then reads the PNG header back and fails if it did not get it. Do NOT use `qlmanage`: it is
a thumbnailer, it pads non-square artwork into a square, and it flattens transparency to white.
Both were measured on 2026-08-27. Every exported raster is rendered and looked at, at final size,
before it ships.
