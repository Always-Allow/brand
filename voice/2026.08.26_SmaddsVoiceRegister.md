## How her posts are built

1. **A momentum opener, never a cold start.** "Welcome back to What We Built
   Wednesdays!", "We're back with another...", "What We Built Wednesdays keeps on
   rolling", "We're back with How We Build Wednesdays".
2. **The problem set up conversationally, often as the questions in her head.** "what
   folder am i in? where am i in the session context? how much am i spending?"; "how do
   we keep our ear to the ground... without the burden on our stakeholders to bring
   them to us...?"
3. **Long, comma-stacked spoken sentences, not short punchy ones.** "It's an easy
   default: the explanation seems to make enough sense, the CLI probably knows more
   than you do, and you want to keep moving with your project." Colons introduce the
   payoff; clauses ride on commas.
4. **Arrow bullets (→) for outputs, bold terms for named things,** numbered or
   emoji-led buckets for structure.
5. **Parenthetical asides, often self-deprecating.** "To the less-technical eye (me)",
   "this (emotional) friction", "delegate and manage (parts) of our Salesforce".
6. **Ellipses as connective tissue.** "....so what would it look like if we could
   'literally' keep our ear to the ground?"; "…so you push back….again and again".
7. **Doubled punctuation for heat, used sparingly.** "!!", "??", "what buckets am i
   missing??"
8. **Lowercase drift when she's being casual.** "the way skills are defined and framed
   ('recipe,' 'rules') wasn't landing for me, it was either too abstract or it put the
   skill in a box".
9. **Real, measured numbers, never vanity ones.** "1 in 6 messages I sent last week",
   "I've used this 49 times in the 3 days since building it", "62 hours of manual
   RevOps work eliminated already".
10. **A principle to close, earned by the story above it.** "I'd rather figure that out
    by running something than by sitting through a demo."; "The systems that stick
    around are the ones that evolve quickly!!"; "it's us treating our own work like a
    product instead of a system of record".

## Words and phrases she actually reuses

- **build / built / building**, constantly; "She builds her way out of it!!"
- **"AI with me" as a verb:** "Three chances to AI with me in July"; (recorded
  elsewhere: "I'm excited to AI with you").
- **time back:** "real time back", "she wanted that time back", "Time back to our
  team".
- **hands-on**, **runs / running / runs without them**, **hand off / hand over**.
- **friction**, **walk in sharp**, **ear to the ground**, **the paper trail**,
  **spiral into**, **eating a real chunk of her week**, **IYKYK**; **"watch the blast
  radius"** and **"build up"** (scheduled).
- Warm sign-offs and invitations: "Seats are free and they go quick, so grab one and
  bring a friend!!"

## What she does not do

No em dashes as a habit. No three-word sentence runs. No "That is the whole pitch"
style meta-declarations. No jargon without an inline gloss ("the WIIFM,
does-this-apply-to-me version"). No fake enthusiasm; the heat lands on specifics.

Added from her v6 bio read (2026-08-26): present tense for who she is and for what
people around her are doing ("I'm not dead"); a setup line orients the reader before
any list starts; no "there are" list constructions; nothing that implies slowness, so
"a quarter" and any project timeline measured in quarters never appear near her name.

Added from her v3 bio read (2026-08-26): no dramatic intensifiers for their own sake
("my whole career" is wrong where "my career" is true); literal beats vivid ("In 2024 I
started handing that work to Cursor and working in terminal," never "at some point I
started handing that work to AI"); no ego-framing ("people started asking me to teach
their teams" is out); no stacked listicle clauses dressed as color ("the data everyone
argues about" is out); the origin story that matters is why she shares what she learns,
never how the brand was born.

## How to use this register

Copy written as her draws its constructions and phrases from the quotes above, not
from an imitation of their vibe. Every her-voice draft cites this file, and a draft
that cannot point to which of her moves it is using is not in her voice yet. This
register grows only from things she actually published; her chat messages are rulings,
not voice data.
