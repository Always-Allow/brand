# Voice map

**Date:** 2026-10-22. Short pointer for agents. Does not replace the guides. If this map and a guide disagree, the guide wins, then Sarah's correction in the current chat.

## The two voices

| Voice | Who speaks | Source of constructions | Default mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Smadds** | Sarah in first person | [Smadds voice register](2026.08.26_SmaddsVoiceRegister.md) | Her published posts. Sparse heat (`!!`) only where the register shows it. |
| **Always Allow** | The company, neutral | [Brand voice guide](2026.08.27_BrandVoiceGuide.md) | No first person. No em dashes. No invented taglines, metaphors, or personality claims. |

Provenance rule stays section 1 of the brand voice guide: if the guide does not give it, ask Sarah or leave it out.

## Which surface uses which

| Surface | Voice | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (personal), What We Built Wednesday, personal recap posts | Smadds | Register first. Cite which of her moves the draft is using. |
| About page, host bio where she is the subject in first person | Smadds | Positioning paragraph voice from the brand guide. |
| Pre-event and post-event email | Smadds | Post-event is the warmest written register; real enthusiasm is correct there. |
| Live teaching, Granola transcripts, cleaned talk scripts meant to sound like her | Smadds, spoken | Exempt from written mechanics. Do not "correct" a talk toward site prose. |
| Site pages (home, offers, partnerships, privacy) | Always Allow | Neutral. Short teasers. Shows rather than declares. |
| SOW, quote, invoice, directory one-liners | Always Allow | Formal, concrete, outcome-priced. Descriptor tagline when a one-line company line is needed. |
| Whitepaper, AEO page speaking as the company, brand social as Always Allow | Always Allow | Answer or claim first, source named. No brochure lists on AEO. |
| Workshop deck body and slide titles | Always Allow framing, her examples | Deck teaches the pattern; her stories stay hers. Slide titles are takeaway sentences (`slide-titles` skill). |
| Luma / event listing description | Event-copy workflow | Conversational, direct. Usually two compact paragraphs plus one bold takeaway. Closer: *[Always Allow](https://alwaysallow.com/) is hands-on AI strategy and training for people who feel like they could be getting more out of AI. If you've ever used AI and thought, "I know I can get more from this," you should join us.* September 25 closer wins over older Luma rows in the brand guide table. |
| Concept brief | Smadds document voice | Written for her and collaborators, not the public site. `concept-brief` skill gates. |
| Workshop handout / one-pager | Always Allow | Brand templates. Her name and bio blocks only from approved people copy. |

## Taglines (job, not mood)

From the brand guide. One line per job:

| Line | Job |
|---|---|
| "See the patterns. Build your systems." | Promise (site sells) |
| "Hands-on AI strategy and training for operators." | Descriptor (formal / business) |
| "ship it anyway" | Rally (closings, last slide, merch) |

Do not revive dead lines named in the brand guide.

## Agent default

If the surface is not in the table, ask which voice before drafting. Do not blend first person and company voice in one piece unless the surface is defined that way (event page host block: company, then Smadds in third person).

Graphics are not voice. Ask Brandy Assets for logos, covers, and platform art.
